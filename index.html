Best family restaurant in Barh: your questions

Everything you need to know about dining, delivery and bookings at Chatkara Family Restaurant, Barh.

Which is the best family restaurant in Barh?

Chatkara Family Restaurant at A.N.S. College, Savera More, Barh is a 5.0-rated family restaurant loved by local families for its Chinese, North Indian, biryani and rolls, friendly service and comfortable dine-in seating.

Does Chatkara Family Restaurant deliver food in Barh?

Yes. We deliver within 3 km of the restaurant for orders above ₹300. Pickup is available for any order. Order online from our menu and confirm on WhatsApp.

What food does Chatkara serve?

Chinese (chilli paneer, chilli chicken, noodles, Manchurian), North Indian curries (paneer, mushroom, chicken and dal), chicken dum biryani, rice, rotis and parathas, soups and rolls. Both veg and non-veg dishes are available.

Where is Chatkara Family Restaurant in Barh?

A.N.S. College, Savera More, Near Biranchi Bihar, Barh (Patna), Bihar 803213. Tap Get Directions on this page to open Google Maps.

Can I book a table or a birthday party at Chatkara?

Yes. Use the Reserve a Table form on this page for table reservations and birthday or party bookings, or call 086770 44213.

How much does food cost at Chatkara Family Restaurant?

Rolls start from ₹30, noodles from ₹35, paneer and chicken curries from about ₹100 for a half plate, and Chicken Dum Biryani is ₹99 (half) or ₹180 (full).
